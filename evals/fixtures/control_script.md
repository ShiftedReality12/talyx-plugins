# Meeting script

## P1 Open
Congratulations on the second close at Alder Grey [5]. One idea on references before your next vehicle [6].

## P2 Beats
- **Open:** the second vehicle and its reference needs [5][10].
- **Credibility in one line:** one custodian introduction that landed in weeks [12].
- **One insight they did not have:** where the close date creates reference pressure [11].
- **Ask for the second meeting:** a 30-minute follow-up with the custodian [12].

## P3 Questions
1. What changed after the second close [8]?
2. How does Alder Grey decide on a new custodian [9]?
3. What would a reference have to show to matter [10]?

## P4 If they say
- "We have references." -> "Then one more from a custodian shortens the close [11]."
- "Not now." -> "Understood; the close date is fixed, so I will send one page first [11]."

## P6 Close and ask
Ask: 30 minutes with the custodian [12]. Fallback: one page by email [12].

## P7 Never say
"No pressure" · "Just checking in"
