# Creative Framework: Talyx Product / Plugin Launch Films (derived from Pika x ChatGPT)

Version 1, 2026-10-16. Reference: `~/Downloads/pcp-refs/pika-chatgpt.mp4`. Reusable for any Talyx plugin or product launch film. Section 12 maps it to PCP 30 s.

## 1. Core idea
A launch film sells a **feeling of capability inside a tool people already use**. Pika's recipe: metaphor hook, bold claim, drop, rebuilt host UI, visible thinking, escalation from simple to magic, scale beat, callback, minimal end card. The camera never rests; color is saved for the payoff.

## 2. Beat structure template

| # | Beat | Purpose | Ratio | 30 s | 60 s |
|---|------|---------|-------|------|------|
| 1 | Logo sting | Brand in 1 beat | 3% | 0.0-0.8 | 0.0-1.5 |
| 2 | Metaphor hook | Visual promise, no UI | 12% | 0.8-4.0 | 1.5-8.5 |
| 3 | Interstitial claim | "WE PUT X / INSIDE Y" | 7% | 4.0-6.0 | 8.5-12.5 |
| 4 | Drop + host UI open | Music drop, pace set | 5% | 6.0-7.5 | 12.5-15.5 |
| 5 | Prompt typed (close-up) | Show the one action | 8% | 7.5-10.0 | 15.5-20.5 |
| 6 | Thinking log | Make the work visible | 10% | 10.0-13.0 | 20.5-26.5 |
| 7 | Escalation / payoff | Simple -> magic, color arrives | 37% | 13.0-24.0 | 26.5-48.5 |
| 8 | Scale beat (optional at 30 s) | "Every X, Y, Z" | 6% | folded into 7 | 48.5-52.0 |
| 9 | Callback to metaphor | Close the loop | 4% | 24.0-25.5 | 52.0-54.5 |
| 10 | End card | Where to get it | 15% | 25.5-30.0 | 54.5-60.0 |

Rules: the drop lands at ~25-30% of runtime (Pika: 9 s of ~35 s). A new idea every 3-5 s. Never hold a beat longer than it takes to read it plus 0.3 s, and never shorter than it takes to read it.

## 3. Hook rules (first 1-5 s)
- Frame 1 must already move. No fade from black longer than 6 frames.
- Hook is a **visual metaphor**, not a quote, not UI, not a talking head.
- The metaphor must be understandable with the sound off by second 2.
- One subject, one motion, one idea. It should end on a hard cut or a snap on the beat.
- Test: a cold viewer, shown 0-2 s only, can say what the product does in roughly 5 words. If not, FAIL.

## 4. In-host UI rules
- Rebuild the host UI (Claude/ChatGPT/Grok) as a clean, stylized vector recreation. No screen recordings, no browser chrome, no OS menu bar, no real account data.
- Monochrome UI (grays, black, white); brand color only in the output/payoff.
- Oversized cursor (2-3x normal), smooth bezier paths, ease-in-out, click ripple on every click.
- Typing ~1.3x real speed, even cadence, zero typos, blinking caret.
- Camera always moving: push into the prompt bar while typing, pan/track to the output, slight drift on holds. No static frames longer than 0.5 s except readable holds (see 7).
- Text in UI must be legible at 1080p on a phone: >= 28 px cap height for anything the viewer must read.

## 5. Thinking-log rules
- 3-5 lines, each <= 5 words, present-progressive ("Reading LinkedIn..."), ending in a positive resolution line ("Found 14 sources").
- Each line appears 0.4-0.7 s apart; a check/tick replaces the spinner as each completes.
- Name real, recognizable sources the viewer trusts. Plain English only.
- Log is the bridge from "typing" to "magic"; it must sit on screen with the prompt still visible or immediately after it.

## 6. Escalation
- Order features from simple to magic; each step bigger than the last.
- Step cadence 3-5 s. Each step introduces one new thing, labeled in <= 3 words.
- The last step is the "money shot" and gets the first full use of color.

## 7. Readability
- Any text the viewer must read: hold time >= 0.25 s per word + 0.3 s, minimum 0.85 s at rest (2D, flat, in focus).
- Motion-in/out does not count toward the hold.

## 8. Typography, color, interstitials
- Interstitials: bold uppercase sans, white on black, hard cuts, 2-6 words per card, 0.6-1.0 s each, cut on the beat.
- One typeface family for the film; weights for hierarchy.
- Palette: monochrome + one brand accent reserved for the payoff and end card.

## 9. Sound and pacing
- Opening: sparse, textured (strings/piano/ambient) under the hook.
- The drop: one hit at the end of the interstitials sets the tempo; cut on beats after the drop.
- SFX: subtle keyboard ticks, click ripples, a "snap" on the convergence. Never louder than the music.
- No voiceover required; the film must work muted.

## 10. End card
- Minimal: logo, one line of where to get it, optional typed command or URL.
- Hold >= 3 s at rest. Motion settles; no new information after the card lands.

## 11. Do / Don't
Do: open on motion; one idea per shot; rebuild UI; make the work visible; save color; callback; work muted.
Don't: open on a quote or a logo-only hold over 1 s; screen-record; show chrome; let the camera die; flash readable text for under 0.85 s; use jargon (military/intel/"dossier/targets/ops"); add new info on the end card; spend color early.

## 12. Pass/fail checklist
1. Frame 1 moves. 2. 0-2 s cold test passes (5-word description). 3. Logo <= 1 s. 4. Interstitials uppercase, <= 6 words, on beat. 5. Drop at 25-30% runtime. 6. Host UI rebuilt, no chrome/recording. 7. Cursor oversized with ripples. 8. Typing faster than real, no typos. 9. Camera never static > 0.5 s outside readable holds. 10. Thinking log 3-5 lines, plain English. 11. New idea every 3-5 s. 12. Monochrome until payoff. 13. Every readable element held >= 0.85 s at rest. 14. Callback present. 15. End card minimal, held >= 3 s. 16. Works muted. 17. No jargon in user-facing copy.
