# Talyx Pre-call Prep -- Gemini Apps Gem

1. In a computer browser open `gemini.google.com` → **Gems** → **New Gem**. Name it **Talyx Pre-call Prep**.
2. Paste all of [gem-instructions.txt](gem-instructions.txt) into **Instructions**.
3. Under **Knowledge**, upload [pcp-knowledge.md](pcp-knowledge.md). **Save**.
4. Open the Gem and ask "Prep me for my call with <Full Name> at <Organisation>." The first time it asks
   the calibration questions and gives you a *Saved setup* block.
5. Paste that block over the one at the end of your copy of `pcp-knowledge.md`, replace the Knowledge
   file in the Gem, and save. New chats then skip calibration.
