# Meeting script
*Facts select the moves; read the room, never the record · Jane Fraser, Citi · Prepared Oct 14, 2026*

## P1 Open
- **Hook:** *Your service-agent AI tools are live in two countries [4], and you say the gains come from scaled use cases applied top down [7]. I want to ask where client meetings sit in that.*
- **Credibility:** I build AI meeting-prep and knowledge tools for executive teams [20].
- **Insight:** the Q3 call named service agents [4] and engineers [8]; client prep for 6,000 multinationals [5] is a gap to test, not a fact.
- **Rule:** keep it to her outcome; return to her words before each question.

## P2 Question order
- **Story:** Where does AI help the bankers meeting your 6,000 multinational clients [5], beyond service agents [4]?
- **Process:** Which scaled use cases, applied top down [7], come next after 14,000 engineers got a coding tool [8]?
- **Gate:** Who shapes AI priorities now that the COO's office has named a group head of AI [9]?
- **History:** What makes a tool worth buying when vendor spend fell 30% [6]?
- **Commitment:** What result by next quarter would move you toward the 11-13% goal [12]?

## P3 Objections: name it, then answer
- **"We build our own AI."** *Your engineers do [8]. I mean client prep, not code.*
- **"Vendor spend is down."** *It is [6]. I am asking for 30 minutes, not a contract.*
- **"Transformation comes first."** *It does [18]. This is one scaled use case, tested with one team [7].*

## P4 Close
- **Restate:** ask what would make a second meeting an easy yes, then say it back in her words.
- **Real deadline:** the Jan 14, 2027 earnings call [19].
- **The ask:** 30 minutes with the owner of client-facing AI · **Fallback:** an introduction to the COO's office.
- **Follow-up:** the one-page sample client brief, by Oct 17.

## P5 Read the room (watch, do not assume)
- **Fast, wants the answer:** state the outcome in one sentence [2].
- **Asks for terms and data:** bring numbers and sources [16].
- **Talks about people:** start from what clients need [14].
- **Big picture, ideas:** place it in year three of the AI shift [13].

## P6 Influence, one fact behind each
- **Reciprocity:** hand over the sample client brief [5].
- **Commitment:** ask her to name one scaled use case [7].
- **Liking:** use her frame: what clients need [14].

## P7 If it stalls
- **The move:** ask what result she wants to report on the Jan 14, 2027 call [19].
- **The move:** ask which client segment she would test first [5].

## P8 Guardrails
- **Rule:** promise no results or dates; use public facts only; skip job cuts.
- **Never say:** "Does that make sense?" · "Is this helpful?" · "No pressure" · "You'd know better than me" · "Just checking in"
