*Facts select the moves; read the room, never the record · Mary Barra, General Motors · Prepared Oct 13, 2026*

- **Hook:** *You told investors that near-term EV adoption will be lower than planned [2], and in January that GM still believes in EVs [6]. I'd like to hear how you hold both.*
- **Credibility:** Talyx builds AI meeting-prep and knowledge tools for executive teams [15].
- **Insight:** This conversation was prepared with a three-page brief where every fact carries its source [15].
- **Rule:** Let the hook land, then ask the first question and stay quiet.

## P2 Question order
- **Story:** When near-term EV adoption fell below plan [2], which GM decisions changed first, and which stayed put?
- **Process:** What does moving Orion to full-size trucks and SUVs from early 2027 [3] change for your leadership team's preparation?
- **Gate:** Who else shapes how GM's senior leaders prepare for outside meetings like this one?
- **History:** You said people are what make General Motors [7]; where do your leaders lose the most time before big decisions?
- **Commitment:** If a 30-minute follow-up after Oct 20 [4] made sense, who from your team would join?

## P3 Objections: name it, then answer
- **Their likely objection:** *"This is the week before we report."* Agreed; I'm asking for time after Oct 20 [4].
- **Their likely objection:** *"We already have a team for this."* Then I'd like to learn what they still lose time on [7].
- **Their likely objection:** *"Why should I spend time on this?"* Because you said to face the brutal facts [14]; I'd show you one.

## P4 Close
- **Restate:** ask what would make a follow-up an easy yes, then say it back in her words.
- **Real deadline:** the Oct 20 report [4], before the December pickup launch [12].
- **The ask:** 30 minutes after Oct 20 with Mary Barra or the person she names · **Fallback:** 15 minutes with that person.
- **Follow-up:** send the side-by-side of her two EV statements [2][6] within a day.

## P7 If it stalls
- **The move:** return to her Jan 2026 line, "We continue to believe in EVs" [6], and ask what the next proof point is.
- **The move:** ask who owns preparation for the December pickup launch [12].

## P8 Guardrails
- **Rule:** promise nothing about GM's results or timing; claim only what the cited record says.
- **Never say:** "Does that make sense?" · "Is this helpful?" · "No pressure" · "You'd know better than me" · "Just checking in"
