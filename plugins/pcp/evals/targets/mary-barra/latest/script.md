# Meeting script
*Facts select the moves; read the room, never the record · Mary Barra, General Motors · Prepared Oct 14, 2026*

## P1 Open
- **Hook:** *You review how your teams use AI every other week [3]. I would like to hear what that has changed.*
- **Credibility:** Talyx builds meeting-prep and knowledge tools for executive teams [15].
- **Insight:** North America margin rose 2.5 points to 8.6% [12] while EV losses are set to fall $1-1.5 billion [7]; prepared decisions compound.
- **Rule:** answer her questions with a headline and one proof point before asking yours.

## P2 Question order
- **Story:** What has the biweekly AI review changed in how your teams prepare for decisions [3]?
- **Process:** How do product and software choices reach you, given the chief product officer reports to Reuss [6]?
- **Gate:** What must you see before the next EV decision, with EV losses due to improve $1-1.5 billion [7]?
- **History:** Where did the skilled-trades AI start-up example spread beyond that one team [5]?
- **Commitment:** Would 30 minutes with your AI review team work better before or after the Oct 20 earnings call [8]?

## P3 Objections: name it, then answer
- **"We are focused on earnings and EV costs."** *Fair; results come Oct 20 [8]. Thirty minutes after that stays off your critical path.*
- **"We have our own AI teams."** *You review them every other week [3]. We fit around their work; we do not replace it.*
- **"Send something first."** *Gladly: one cited page on GM's public record, like the guidance raise to $14-16 billion [2].*

## P4 Close
- **Restate:** ask what would make a second meeting an easy yes, then say it back in her words.
- **Real deadline:** Q3 earnings on Oct 20, 2026 [8].
- **The ask:** 30 minutes with her AI review team after Oct 20 · **Fallback:** 15 minutes with its lead.
- **Follow-up:** the one-page example within two days.

## P5 Read the room (watch, do not assume)
- **Fast, wants the answer:** headline first, one proof point such as 42% pickup share [13].
- **Asks for terms and data:** bring the numbers, such as the 8.6% margin [12].
- **Talks about people:** follow her lead on the small team she reviews with [3].
- **Big picture, ideas:** tie to safety as the overriding priority [14].

## P7 If it stalls
- **The move:** ask how product choices reach her, given the chief product officer reports to Reuss [6].

## P8 Guardrails
- **Rule:** promise no savings, results or accuracy; make no claims about competitors; cite only public sources.
- **Never say:** "Does that make sense?" · "Is this helpful?" · "No pressure" · "You'd know better than me" · "Just checking in"
