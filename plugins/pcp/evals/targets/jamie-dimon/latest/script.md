# Meeting script
*Facts select the moves; read the room, never the record · Jamie Dimon, JPMorgan Chase · Prepared Oct 13, 2026*

## P1 Open
- **Hook:** *You told Davos JPMorgan Chase has 500 AI use cases and "you got to get better at it" [4]. I'd like to show you one place that gets better.*
- **Credibility:** I'm from Talyx; we make AI meeting-prep for executive teams [13].
- **Insight:** Your April letter says AI will enhance some jobs [3]; preparing for meetings is one.
- **Rule:** stop after the hook and let him answer first.

## P2 Question order
- **Story:** Dimon's letter says AI will eliminate some jobs while enhancing others [3]; which JPMorgan Chase roles get redeployed first?
- **Process:** You told Davos JPMorgan Chase has 500 AI use cases [4]; which one still lacks a clear measure of results?
- **Gate:** Who besides Dimon decides which tools his executive team at JPMorgan Chase tries?
- **History:** Dimon may stay about three more years [2]; what must his successor inherit from how he prepares?
- **Commitment:** What would make a second 30-minute meeting worth Dimon's time?

## P3 Objections: name it, then answer
- **"JPMorgan Chase already has its own AI."** *Yes, 150,000 people use it weekly [4]. This adds sourced preparation for one meeting.*
- **"I have no time."** *Thirty minutes, once; I send one page first [13].*
- **"AI is cutting jobs here."** *Your letter says it enhances others [3]; this makes preparation better, not smaller.*

## P4 Close
- **Restate:** ask what would make it an easy yes, then say it back in his words.
- **The ask:** a second 30-minute meeting with Dimon or whoever prepares his meetings [13] · **Fallback:** 30 minutes with the executive who owns AI adoption at JPMorgan Chase.
- **Follow-up:** one sample page, the next day.

## P7 If it stalls
- **The move:** return to his words: "you got to get better at it" [4].
- **The move:** ask how a handover in about three years changes how his team prepares [2].

## P8 Guardrails
- **Rule:** promise no results at JPMorgan Chase; make no performance or comparative claims.
- **Never say:** "Does that make sense?" · "Is this helpful?" · "No pressure" · "You'd know better than me" · "Just checking in"
