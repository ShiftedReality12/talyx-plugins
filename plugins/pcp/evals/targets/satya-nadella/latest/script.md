# Meeting script
*Facts select the moves; read the room, never the record · Satya Nadella, Microsoft · Prepared Oct 13, 2026*

## P1 Open
- **Hook:** *You said the value creation opportunity in the agent world is "10X more" [6]. I want to ask what that looks like for executives at Microsoft.*
- **Credibility:** Talyx builds AI meeting-prep and knowledge tools for executive teams [11].
- **Insight:** Microsoft's June quarter showed Azure up 43% [4]; the open question is whether executives are briefed as well as customers are served.
- **Rule:** after the hook, ask and stop; let him fill the silence.

## P2 Question order
- **Story:** When Nadella says tokens into business results, what does that look like for Microsoft's executive teams [5]?
- **Process:** Which of Microsoft's agent platforms would an outside tool plug into, given his 10X remark [6]?
- **Gate:** Who at Microsoft owns a decision on a new executive-team tool?
- **History:** How did the voluntary retirement program change how Microsoft's teams prepare for meetings [9]?
- **Commitment:** What would make a second 30-minute conversation worth his time?

## P3 Objections: name it, then answer
- **Their likely objection:** *"Microsoft builds this itself." Probably true for the platform; we start with one executive team's meetings, which a platform rarely covers [6].*
- **Their likely objection:** *"We are cutting back." Then the question is cost per meeting, in line with turning tokens into business results [5].*
- **Their likely objection:** *"Talk to my team." Gladly; who owns executive tooling at Microsoft [9]?*

## P4 Close
- **Restate:** ask what would make a second meeting an easy yes, then say it back in his words.
- **Real deadline:** Ignite, Nov 17-20, 2026, is a natural date to follow up, not his deadline [10].
- **The ask:** 30 minutes with him or whoever owns executive tooling, within a month · **Fallback:** a named person at Microsoft to talk to.
- **Follow-up:** send a one-page example of a briefed executive team within two days.

## P7 If it stalls
- **The move:** ask how he wants every customer to turn tokens into business results, and where executives fit [5].
- **The move:** ask what the frontier access he describes means inside Microsoft [7].

## P8 Guardrails
- **Rule:** make no promise about results or savings you cannot show; claim nothing about Microsoft's plans beyond its public statements.
- **Never say:** "Does that make sense?" · "Is this helpful?" · "No pressure" · "You'd know better than me" · "Just checking in"

## P9 Next step
**Next step:** send the one-page example by Oct 17, 2026 [11].
