# Quality checks

Every brief passes all eight checks before hand-over. A failed check means rewrite that section, then check again.

| # | Check | Passes when |
|---|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Swap test | Putting another person's name on any section would make it wrong. A section that still reads true for someone else is too generic. |
| 2 | Source check | Every fact in sections 2, 3 and 6 has an entry in the source log. No invented figures, deals, titles or dates. |
| 3 | Quote check | Every quote is exact, in quotation marks, and sourced. If there are no quotes, the card says so. |
| 4 | Custom question | At least one discovery question is written for this person alone and names something from research. |
| 5 | Completeness | All ten sections are present, in order. |
| 6 | Length | Openers two sentences or fewer; each question 50 words or fewer; the framing in section 6 is 120 words or fewer. Trim; do not split. |
| 7 | No approval-seeking | None of the "never ask" phrases in section 8 appears anywhere else in the brief. |
| 8 | Outcome, not method | Section 6 states what changes for them, not how the user's work is built. A competitor reading it learns nothing they could copy. |

## Research status

- **FULL**: at least one confirmed fact from each of passes 1, 3 and 4.
- **LIMITED**: anything less. The header says so and the hand-over table flags it.
