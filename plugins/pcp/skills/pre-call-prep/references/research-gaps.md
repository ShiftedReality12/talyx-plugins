# Research gaps

What to do when research does not go to plan. In every case, proceed with what is confirmed and say what is missing.

| Situation | What to do |
|-----------|------------|
| A URL fails (403, 404, empty) | Search for the same content. Tag facts found that way `[SEARCH]`. If nothing turns up, record the gap. |
| LinkedIn is behind a login | Search for the career history on the organisation's bio page, conference pages or press coverage. If nothing turns up, use the intake data only. |
| No recent news | Use older confirmed facts. In the brief, write: "No recent public developments found. Ask what they are working on now." |
| No direct quotes | Replace the "In their words" card with "Themes from their public presence", built from their bio and organisation pages. Say that no direct quotes were found. |
| Two or more people match | **Stop.** Show the user the matches with the details that tell them apart (organisation, city, title). Wait for the user to confirm. Never pick one yourself. |
| Almost nothing found | Write a `LIMITED` brief from the intake data only. Mark the header `[LIMITED RESEARCH: prepare by hand before the meeting]`. Use the standard discovery questions. Flag the person in the hand-over table. |

## Decision order

1. Is the person confirmed as the right person? If not, stop and ask.
2. Is there at least one confirmed fact beyond the intake? If not, write a `LIMITED` brief.
3. Otherwise, write the full brief and list every gap in the header.
