# Research protocol

Four passes per person. Public sources only. For 2 or more people, run the passes for each person in parallel.

## Pass 1: Identity

- Search: `"<Full Name>" "<Organisation>" <Title>`
- Extract: current role and start date, prior roles, education and credentials, board seats, awards, published work.
- Look in: the organisation's own bio page, press releases, conference speaker pages, industry publications, public regulatory registers relevant to their profession.

## Pass 2: Links

- Fetch every URL in the intake row.
- If a page fails (403, 404, login wall), search for the same content and tag facts found that way as `[SEARCH]`.
- A LinkedIn page behind a login counts as failed. Search for `"<Full Name>" <Organisation> career` instead.

## Pass 3: Organisation

- Search: `"<Organisation>" news OR announces OR partnership OR acquisition <current year>`
- Extract: leadership changes, deals, new offices, launches, growth or contraction signals, anything the person led.
- Note the organisation's own description of itself, in its own words, from its website.

## Pass 4: Voice

- Search: `"<Full Name>" interview OR podcast OR article OR panel OR quoted`
- Extract: direct quotes, recurring themes, the words they use for their own work, the problems they talk about.
- Record exact quotes with the source URL. Paraphrase is labelled as paraphrase.

## Communication style

From the voice pass, note how the person communicates, with the evidence:

| Style | Signals | In the meeting |
|-------|---------|----------------|
| Direct | Short answers, results first, decisive language | Get to the point. Lead with the outcome. |
| Expressive | Stories, energy, people and recognition | Let them talk. Use examples and names they will know. |
| Steady | Careful, loyal, process and people-first | Build trust. Reduce risk. Give time to decide. |
| Analytical | Data, precision, detail, sources | Bring evidence. Be exact. Allow silence after a question. |

If there is too little evidence to read a style, say so. Do not guess.

## Research file

Compile one research file per person before writing the brief:

1. Career summary (sourced)
2. Organisation summary and recent developments (sourced)
3. Quotes or public themes (sourced)
4. Communication style with evidence
5. Gaps: what you looked for and could not find
6. Source log: every fact, its URL, and `[DIRECT]` or `[SEARCH]`
