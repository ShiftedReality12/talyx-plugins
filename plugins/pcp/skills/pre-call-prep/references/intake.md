# Intake

One row per person. The CSV header is in `../assets/intake-template.csv`.

## Required

| Field | Why it matters |
|-------|----------------|
| Full Name | The research anchor. Include a middle initial if the name is common. |
| Organisation | Separates this person from others with the same name. |
| Meeting Date | Sets the window for "recent" news. |
| Meeting Objective | Decides the meeting type and what the brief builds toward. |

## Recommended

| Field | Why it matters |
|-------|----------------|
| Title/Role | Confirms the right person and shapes the questions. |
| Relationship | How you know them: new introduction, referral, colleague, existing client. |
| Relationship Length | Decides the order of the opening (personal first or professional first). |
| LinkedIn URL | Direct source for career history. |
| Organisation Website | Direct source for the organisation's own words. |
| Additional URL 1-3 | Articles, talks, filings or bios you already know about. |

## Optional

| Field | Why it matters |
|-------|----------------|
| Known Priorities | What you already know they care about. Research tests it; it is not assumed true. |
| Icebreaker Notes | Shared history or context worth opening with. |
| Specific Ask | The one thing you want to ask for. Shapes the close. |
| Notes | Anything else. |

## Missing fields

- No full name or no organisation: ask before starting. Do not guess.
- No meeting objective: ask one question, "What do you want to walk out of this meeting with?"
- Any other gap: proceed, and note the gap in the brief header.
